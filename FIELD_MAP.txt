Panels field map

Panels browser setup, graphs and Capture/replay: see PANELS_SETUP.txt.

1. Build and fully install the updated APK after the Panels migration.
   Later code-only changes can use the normal Sloth reload.
2. Connect your computer to the robot's Wi-Fi and open:
   http://192.168.43.1:8001
3. Select Field Map (Diagnostics) on Driver Station and press INIT.
   Add the Field and Telemetry widgets to your Panels layout.
4. In Panels Configurables > FieldMap, enter startX, startY, and
   startHeadingDegrees for the measured center of your robot.
   Coordinates are inches from the bottom-left corner with the audience below:
   X increases to the right; Y increases away from the audience.
   Heading 0 points right, 90 points up, 180 left, and 270 toward the audience.
5. Keep the robot stationary. If calibration is needed, press Square during INIT
   and wait for Pinpoint READY. Press Cross during INIT to apply the starting pose.
   Editing the values alone does not reset the robot's position.
6. Press START and move the robot by hand. Field Map does not power the drive
   motors. For powered driving, use Skeleton TeleOp, which retains the last
   pose and shows the same map automatically.

The teal circle is the estimated robot center; its line points forward.
The trail shows up to 10 seconds of recent motion, refreshing at 10 Hz.
A pose reset clears the trail. Invalid/calibrating localization immediately
hides the robot marker, clears the trail, and reports POSITION UNAVAILABLE in
Telemetry. Field X/Y, heading, and localization status appear there every loop.

Panels uses its bundled BIOBUZZ field image with a BIOBUZZ / Pedro preset.
Keep that incoming preset and remove saved manual coordinate overrides if the
Field widget displays a rotated/flipped view. Red is left, blue is right, and
the audience is below. The robot circle marks position, not its measured
footprint. The image does not display live HIVE tilt or moving obstacles.

Position comes from Pedro/Pinpoint odometry. The starting pose, pod settings,
and calibration must match the real robot; the map cannot discover its
absolute location. OpModes that reset pose to zero show motion relative to
that zero until their actual field starting pose is supplied.

All OpModes using the shared Drivetrain publish this map during their normal
localization updates, including Skeleton TeleOp/autos and Hive Pedro Test.
The standalone Pinpoint Check and Pedro AutoTune use their own loops and do
not publish this map. Use Field Map for the standalone display.
